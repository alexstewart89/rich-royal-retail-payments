What the €973 may not include

Transaction-based pricing usually sits alongside other charges on a card acquirer's statement. Worth checking your First Cash Solutions contract for: monthly terminal rental per device, a service or support fee, a PCI compliance fee, minimum monthly charges, and the notice period or exit charge. Shopify has none of these, so every euro you find there narrows or closes the gap above.

4. What changes at the checkout

There are two ways to take card payments with Shopify POS. Option 1, keep your First Cash terminals: staff enter the sale in Shopify POS, then type the same amount into the First Cash terminal, and at the end of the day the two totals are compared by hand. Option 2, Shopify Retail Payments: the card reader is connected to Shopify POS, so the amount is sent to the reader automatically and the payment is recorded against the sale on its own. The points below describe what Option 2 changes for your stores.

01One tap to take payment

With your First Cash terminals: every sale is keyed twice, once in POS and once on the terminal.

With a Shopify reader the amount goes straight from the basket to the card reader. No typing, no mis-keyed €89.00 as €98.00, no "paid on terminal but not in POS" at close. On a busy Saturday in Stuttgart that is seconds saved on every single transaction, and no differences to investigate on Monday.

Removes the double entry and the mismatches it creates.

02Returns and exchanges back to the original card, in any store

With your First Cash terminals: the refund is processed separately on the terminal, and only where the terminal account allows it.

A customer who bought in Leipzig and returns in Wolfsburg is refunded to their original card from the order in POS, in one step. Exchanges settle the difference automatically. The refund shows against the original order in Shopify, so finance sees one record, not two.

Included with POS Pro, which you already have.

03One daily payout and no month-end matching

With your First Cash terminals: a First Cash statement to reconcile against POS totals, store by store, every month.

Card sales from the four stores and the online shop settle into the same Shopify Payments account you have used since 2023, in one daily EUR payout, broken down by location, tender and order in the Finance section of the admin. The reconciliation is already done when the money lands.

Same account, same bank, same reporting your finance team already knows.

04Hardware that fits the shop floor

With your First Cash terminals: readers tied to the counter, and no link between the device and the sale.

The Verifone Victa Mobil is a handheld that runs Shopify POS, scans barcodes and takes the payment in one device, so staff can complete a sale anywhere in the store during busy periods instead of sending customers to queue at the counter. The POS Terminal gives a countertop setup with a customer-facing display. Both are one-off purchases from the Shopify Hardware Store, shipped to your head office within days.

From €59 per store with the WisePad 3; €299 for the handheld.

05A rate that behaves the same on a €40 basket and a €400 basket

With transaction-based fees: small outlet baskets carry a proportionally higher cost; large ones a lower one.

The approved rates are a flat percentage with no fixed fee per transaction. Your effective cost stays at 1.00% whether a customer buys one item or a full outfit, and it does not change in a quieter month. It is simpler to budget and simpler to explain to the director.

Predictable: €1.00 on every €100, nothing else.

06Security and compliance handled

With a separate acquirer: a second set of PCI obligations and a second support line.

Shopify is PCI DSS Level 1 certified and the readers are EMV certified, so card data never touches your tablets or your network. Fraud screening, 3D Secure online and dispute handling in store sit in one admin, with one 24/7 support team for POS and payments together.

One provider to call when something goes wrong at the checkout.

07Ready for November

The risk: a payments decision that slips the POS rollout into peak trading.

Shopify Payments is already active on your account for online sales, so retail is a setting to switch on, not a new application. The approved rates are applied on activation, readers ship from the German hardware store in days, and your tablets are already in place. There is no contract to sign and nothing to cancel if you later change your mind.

Activation can be done the same week as the POS go-live.

5. Things to check before you decide

Four points worth confirming so there are no surprises after go-live. We can help with each.

  • girocard. Shopify readers process Visa, Mastercard, Maestro and Amex, including girocards that carry a Visa or Mastercard co-badge (most cards issued since 2023). A girocard with no co-badge cannot be taken on a Shopify reader. Your First Cash statements should show how much of your volume, if any, runs over the girocard network alone; we will help you read them.
  • American Express. You do not accept Amex today. On Shopify readers all supported brands are on by default and cannot be switched off individually, so Amex would be accepted at 1.20%. For most fashion retailers that is welcome; worth knowing in advance.
  • First Cash Solutions contract. Notice period, any exit charge, and whether terminal rental or service fees are billed separately from the €973 of transaction fees. These decide the real size of the gap in section 2.
  • Volume for a full year. The approved rates were set on summer data. If a full year of card volume is materially above €1M, it is worth telling us, as that is the threshold our finance team uses for a fuller pricing review.
